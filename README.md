# Arkline Projects 

Arkline Projects needed a website that could clearly show potential customers what they do and the kind of work they offer. Their services cover carpentry, cabinetry, renovations and interior solutions, but without an online presence, it was harder for people to discover their work and understand the process behind it.

I built a clean, responsive website using **HTML, CSS and JavaScript** to give the company a professional online presence. The site presents their services, showcases their work and makes it easier for potential clients to understand what Arkline Projects offers — from measuring and planning to workshop production and final installation.
