SHEET 02 / STUDIO

Who's holding the pencil.

A short brief on how we work before you look at the scope of work.

ArkLine Projects turns your ideas into beautiful, functional spaces tailored to your needs, style and budget. We provide quality carpentry, cabinetry, renovations and interior solutions — measured on site, drawn up, then built in the workshop before it's fitted into your home.

We're committed to quality workmanship and attention to detail, from the first tape-measure mark to the final wipe-down. If a hinge doesn't close right or an edge doesn't sit flush, we go back and fix it before we call a job finished.
